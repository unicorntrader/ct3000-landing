CoTrader reads your Interactive Brokers trade history through a Flex Query, a read-only report that IBKR generates on a schedule. Setup takes about five minutes, you do it once, and nothing in the process can place a trade. This guide walks through all of it.

CoTrader connects to one broker only, and that is on purpose. IBKR gives us clean, complete fill data: every entry, every exit, every commission, timestamped accurately. Callouts and your Weekly Review are only as good as the data behind them, and a trade history that's "mostly accurate" produces insights that are only "mostly useful." Going deep on one broker beat supporting a dozen at a shallower level.

Read-only, on purpose

CoTrader can see your trade history. It cannot place, modify, or cancel a single order. The Flex Query token you create below pulls one report and nothing else. It carries no trading permission of any kind, so your actual trading keeps happening in IBKR, exactly where it already does.

What you'll need

  • An Interactive Brokers account with some trade history.
  • IBKR Client Portal access, the browser login at interactivebrokers.com. These steps are not in TWS.
  • About five minutes. You do this once.

By the end you'll have two values from IBKR, a Query ID and a token, pasted into CoTrader.

In IBKR Client Portal

  1. Log in at interactivebrokers.com.
  2. Open Performance & Reports in the top menu, then the Flex Queries tab.
  1. In the Activity Flex Query panel, click +. Name the query CT3000 Activity.
  2. Under Sections, tick Trades, Open Positions, Account Information and Corporate Actions.
  3. Open each of those four and click Select All.
  1. Under Delivery Configuration, set Format to XML and Period to Last 30 Calendar Days.
  2. Under General Configuration, leave the date and time formats at their defaults (yyyyMMdd, HHmmss, ; semi-colon). Save the query.
  1. Open the saved query's details and copy the Query ID, the number next to the name. That's your first value.
  1. Back on the Flex Queries page, click the gear on the Flex Web Service Configuration panel.
  2. Tick Flex Web Service Status, click Save, and copy the token (a long string of digits). That's your second value.
Token expiry: IBKR expires the token on the schedule you pick when you enable the service. If it lapses, come back here and generate a new one.

In CoTrader

  1. Open Settings and choose Manage IBKR connection.
  1. Paste in the Query ID and the token.
  2. Click Test Connection, then Save & Sync. Your last 30 days import right away.

How the sync works after that

The Flex Query is a rolling 30-day window. In practice:

  • On connect: CoTrader imports your last 30 days of trades.
  • Every day after: it re-runs the query, which always returns the most recent 30 days, and adds anything new to your CoTrader history.
  • Over time: your record in CoTrader keeps growing, because CoTrader holds on to everything it has already pulled even though IBKR only ever serves the last month.

The one gap: trades from more than 30 days before you connected. IBKR will not return them through this query, so they stay in IBKR and don't appear in CoTrader.

After you're connected

Once that first 30 days is in, the rest of CoTrader starts working straight away. Callouts can scan those trades immediately, and your first Weekly Review already has real numbers behind it instead of a week of waiting. Setup is the only manual step. The sync runs on its own from then on, roughly once a day.

If a sync doesn't land

  • Test Connection fails: re-check that the token and Query ID pasted cleanly with no trailing spaces, and that the Flex Web Service is still switched on in IBKR.
  • A recent trade is missing: confirm the Trades section is set to Select All, then give it a day, since IBKR regenerates the report on its own schedule.
  • Older trades never appeared: expected. The query only reaches back 30 days, and your history builds forward from the day you connected.
