CoTrader reads your Interactive Brokers trade history through a Flex Query, a read-only report that IBKR generates on a schedule. Setup takes about five minutes, you do it once, and nothing in the process can place a trade. This guide walks through all of it.

CoTrader connects to one broker only, and that is on purpose. IBKR gives us clean, complete fill data: every entry, every exit, every commission, timestamped accurately. Callouts and your Weekly Review are only as good as the data behind them, and a trade history that's "mostly accurate" produces insights that are only "mostly useful." Going deep on one broker beat supporting a dozen at a shallower level.

Read-only, on purpose

CoTrader can see your trade history. It cannot place, modify, or cancel a single order. There's no execution path in the app at all: nothing to click that touches your account's buying power.

Skipping execution was a deliberate call. A journal that can also trade carries a much bigger blast radius when something goes wrong: a bug, a bad click, a stale session. With read-only access the worst case is a number that looks off and gets corrected. Your actual trading keeps happening in IBKR, exactly where it already does.

The Flex Query token you create below can pull one report and nothing else. It carries no trading permission of any kind.

What you'll need

  • An Interactive Brokers account with some trade history.
  • Access to IBKR Client Portal, the browser login at interactivebrokers.com. These steps are not in TWS.
  • About five minutes.

By the end you'll have two values from IBKR, a Query ID and a token, which you paste into CoTrader once. Steps 1 to 3 happen in IBKR. Step 4 is in CoTrader.

Step 1: Create the Flex Query in IBKR

In Client Portal, open Performance & Reports in the top menu, then Flex Queries.

Next to Activity Flex Query, click + and give the new query a name you'll recognize later, such as CT3000 Activity.

Then set it up like this:

  1. Under Sections, tick Trades, Open Positions, Account Information and Corporate Actions.
  2. Open each of those four sections and choose Select All, so no fields are left out.
  3. Under Delivery Configuration, set Format to XML and Period to Last 30 Calendar Days.
  4. Under General Configuration, leave Date Format as yyyyMMdd, Time Format as HHmmss and Date/Time Separator as ; (semi-colon). These are the defaults, and CoTrader reads them.
  5. Save the query.

Step 2: Turn on the Flex Web Service and copy the token

The Flex Web Service is what lets CoTrader fetch the report by itself, so there is no export step on your side.

Back on the Flex Queries page, find the Flex Web Service Configuration panel and click the gear icon on it.

  1. Tick Flex Web Service Status, then Save.
  2. Copy the token that appears. It is a long string of digits. Keep it handy for Step 4.
Token expiry: IBKR ties the token to your account and expires it on the schedule you pick when you enable the service. If it lapses, come back to this page and generate a new one.

Step 3: Copy the Query ID

Back on the Flex Queries page, click the query you just made to open its details. The Query ID is the number at the top, next to the query name. That's your second value.

Step 4: Paste both into CoTrader

In CoTrader, open Settings and choose Manage IBKR connection.

  1. Paste the Query ID and the token into their fields.
  2. Click Test Connection. CoTrader asks IBKR for the report and confirms the two values work together.

That's the whole setup. You never open this screen again unless the token expires.

How the sync works after that

The Flex Query is a rolling 30-day window. In practice:

  • On connect: CoTrader imports your last 30 days of trades.
  • Every day after: it re-runs the query, which always returns the most recent 30 days, and adds anything new to your CoTrader history.
  • Over time: your record in CoTrader keeps growing, because CoTrader holds on to everything it has already pulled even though IBKR only ever serves the last month.

The one gap: trades from more than 30 days before you connected. IBKR will not return them through this query, so they stay in IBKR and don't appear in CoTrader.

After you're connected

Once that first 30 days is in, the rest of CoTrader starts working straight away. Callouts can scan those trades immediately, and your first Weekly Review already has real numbers behind it instead of a week of waiting. Setup is the only manual step. The sync runs on its own from then on, roughly once a day.

If a sync doesn't land

  • Test Connection fails: re-check that the token and Query ID pasted cleanly with no trailing spaces, and that the Flex Web Service is still switched on in IBKR.
  • A recent trade is missing: confirm the Trades section is set to Select All, then give it a day, since IBKR regenerates the report on its own schedule.
  • Older trades never appeared: expected. The query only reaches back 30 days, and your history builds forward from the day you connected.
