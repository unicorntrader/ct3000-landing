Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

This page explains what CoTrader 3000 ("CoTrader," "we," "us") collects when you use the app, why we collect it, and how you can control it. If anything here is unclear, email us at thinker@philoinvestor.com and a real person will answer.

What we collect

  • Account information — the email and any profile details you give us when you sign up.
  • Trade data from Interactive Brokers — when you connect your IBKR account, we pull your trade history (fills, symbols, timestamps, P&L) through a read-only connection. We never receive your IBKR login credentials directly, and we cannot place, modify, or cancel trades.
  • Usage data — basic, privacy-first product analytics via Plausible, which does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites. We use it to understand which parts of the app are actually useful, not to build an advertising profile.

How we use it

Your trade data powers the features you're actually using CoTrader for: the Journal, Weekly Review, and Callouts. It is used to generate insights about your own trading — nothing more. We do not sell your data, and we do not share your trade history with advertisers or data brokers.

Who else sees it

  • Interactive Brokers — governs the connection itself under its own agreement with you; we only receive the data IBKR's API exposes to a read-only connection.
  • Service providers we rely on to run the product (hosting, email delivery, analytics) see only what's necessary to provide their service, and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Security

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll notify affected users if we ever have reason to believe that promise has been broken.

Your choices

You can disconnect your IBKR account at any time from within the app. You can request that we delete your account and associated data entirely by emailing thinker@philoinvestor.com.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and change the effective date above.
