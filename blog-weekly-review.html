Reading your Weekly Review

3 min read

Every Sunday — or whenever you sit down to do it — CoTrader gives you one screen: Net P&L, win rate, average trade, all for the week that just closed. It takes about two minutes to read. That's the point.

Most traders review their performance the way most people review their diet: sporadically, emotionally, and usually only right after something went badly wrong. The Weekly Review exists so you look at the same three numbers on the same cadence regardless of how the week went — especially when it went fine, because that's when the drift that causes next month's bad week is actually happening.

The three numbers, and what they're really telling you

Net P&L is the outcome. It matters, but it's the least useful number for changing your behavior — a green week can hide a bad process that got lucky, and a red week can hide a good process that had a rough five days. Don't stop here.

Win rate tells you about your hit rate, not your edge. A 40% win rate with a 3:1 reward-to-risk ratio is a great week. A 70% win rate where your losers are three times the size of your winners is a slow leak. Win rate only means something next to average trade.

Average trade is expectancy in miniature — what you actually made or lost per trade, on average, across everything you did that week. This is the number that tells you whether your process is working, independent of how any single trade turned out.

The ritual matters more than any single week

One week of data won't tell you much. Four weeks in a row will. The value of the Weekly Review isn't in any individual screen — it's in the pattern that shows up when you cycle back through the last month and watch these three numbers drift, or hold steady, or improve. CoTrader lets you flip back through prior weeks for exactly this reason.

Two minutes, one screen, every week. It's not a lot to ask, and it's usually the difference between a trader who's improving and one who's just trading.
