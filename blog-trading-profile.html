Setting up your Trading Profile

4 min read

Your Trading Profile is where you tell CoTrader your own rules: how much you're willing to risk on a trade, how big a losing day is too big, what kind of year you're aiming for. Once it's set, CoTrader flags the trades and days that break those rules so you notice the pattern instead of explaining it away.

It's optional, it takes about two minutes, and nothing you enter here can stop a trade. CoTrader is read-only. These are flags after the fact, not guardrails in the moment.

Where to find it

Open the menu from the top right, then Trading → Trading Profile. The screen is a single card of six numbers and a Save button. Every field is a plain number. The $ and % next to them are just labels.

The six numbers

  • Account size. Total capital in the account. Everything else is a percentage of this one figure, so if you leave it blank, none of the flags below do anything.
  • Max risk / trade. The share of your account you're willing to lose on a single trade, as a percent. This is the field that does the most work.
  • Ideal risk / trade. Your target risk per trade. CoTrader only uses this if you leave Max risk empty, as a fallback. Set Max and this one just sits there for your own reference.
  • Ideal daily volatility. The daily account swing you're comfortable with, as a percent. A day that loses more than this gets a Bad day flag.
  • Drawdown appetite. The most you can stand to lose in a single day. A bigger daily loss gets a Drawdown breach flag. Set it above your ideal daily volatility: one means "worse than I'd like," the other means "this really hurt." It's measured on a single day's net loss, not a peak-to-trough equity drop.
  • Return / year target. Your yearly return goal. This one is a number to write down for yourself. CoTrader doesn't measure anything against it yet.

Hit Save profile when you're done. You can come back and change any of it whenever.

What it flags

Once a sync has processed your trades, CoTrader marks the ones that crossed your lines.

On individual trades, in your Smart Journal:

  • Overtraded: a planned trade whose intended risk, from your entry to your planned stop, worked out to more than your Max risk.
  • Big loss: any losing trade whose actual loss came in above your Max risk.

On days, as pills on the day header in the Daily view:

  • Bad day: the day's net loss cleared your ideal daily volatility.
  • Drawdown breach: the day's net loss cleared your drawdown appetite.
  • Over-risk: the day held at least one trade that got flagged.

When a Big loss or Overtraded flag lands, it also drops a line in your Home activity feed with the numbers behind it.

Good to know

  • It only counts from when you set it up. The first save grades your recent history back to that day and no further. Trades and days from before never get flagged.
  • Raising a limit later doesn't un-flag old trades. The trade tagger only adds, it never removes, so trades flagged under the old number keep their flag. Day pills for roughly the last month do recalculate on the next sync.
  • You can dismiss any flag. Delete the tag and it stays gone, even after a re-sync.
